# 📘 RPA Exam Pattern (Robotic Process Automation Certification)

This document provides the complete exam pattern for a typical RPA certification. Use it for quick revision and training repository documentation.

# 📝 1. Exam Overview

Exam Type: Multiple Choice Questions (MCQ)

Total Questions: 50

Duration: 90 minutes

Passing Score: 70%

You must answer 35 out of 50 questions correctly

Exam Mode:

Online (remote proctored)

Offline (test center)

# 📚 2. Question Format

All questions are objective MCQs.

Each question carries 1 mark.

No negative marking.

Questions cover RPA concepts, tools, and real-world automation scenarios.

# 🧠 3. Knowledge Levels Tested

Questions test different cognitive levels:

Level	Description
K1	Recall (definitions, terms, basic RPA concepts)
K2	Understanding (explain RPA processes, bots, workflows)
K3	Application (apply concepts to automation scenarios)
K4	Analysis (analyze processes, troubleshoot bots, design solutions)
# 📌 4. Syllabus Chapters & Weightage
Chapter	Topic	Approx. % of Exam	Weight (Questions)
1	Introduction to RPA	15%	8 questions
2	RPA Tools & Platforms	20%	10 questions
3	Process Identification & Discovery	15%	8 questions
4	Workflow Design & Development	25%	13 questions
5	Exception Handling & Debugging	10%	5 questions
6	RPA Governance, Security & Compliance	10%	5 questions
7	Integration with AI/ML & Advanced Automation	5%	1 question
# 🧪 5. Exam Difficulty Distribution

Easy (K1/K2): ~50%

Moderate (K2/K3): ~35%

Challenging (K3/K4): ~15%

# 🪜 6. RPA Exam Process

Register with the RPA certification provider (UiPath, Automation Anywhere, Blue Prism, or other recognized boards).

Select the exam mode: online or test center.

Schedule your exam date and time slot.

Attempt the exam and submit answers within the allocated duration.

Receive instant or emailed results depending on the provider.

Obtain a digital certificate upon passing.

# 🎯 7. Preparation Tips

Study the RPA tool documentation and glossary carefully.

Practice scenario-based questions simulating real business processes.

Understand attended vs unattended automation and exception handling.

Review workflows, queues, selectors, and integration concepts.

Take mock exams to improve speed, accuracy, and confidence.

Explore RPA governance, security, and compliance best practices.
