# 📘 RPA (Robotic Process Automation) – Tutorial

A beginner-friendly, interview-oriented, real-world RPA tutorial designed for learners preparing to become RPA Developers, Analysts, or Automation Specialists.

# 🧩 1. What Is RPA?

Robotic Process Automation (RPA) is the use of software robots or “bots” to automate repetitive, rule-based tasks in business processes.

It enables faster execution, reduces errors, and allows professionals to focus on higher-value tasks.

Certification in RPA validates that you understand automation fundamentals, tool usage, and process design concepts.
